01. Academic & Social Context

Problem Statement & Domain Challenges

Why traditional social work portals fail to scale effectively in emergency scenarios.

⚠️

1. Fragmented NGO Ecosystem

Over 3.4 million NGOs operate in India in isolation. Lack of a unified digital platform leads to duplicate relief operations and unverified non-profit credentials.

⚡

2. Manual Volunteer Allocation

Volunteers are assigned randomly without skill vector matching. Technical, medical, or educational skills remain unutilized in critical field campaigns.

🕒

3. Delayed Emergency Response

Beneficiary requests queue chronologically rather than by NLP-evaluated urgency score. Severe crisis tickets get buried under standard requests.

🧾

4. Lack of Donor Transparency & Tax Friction

Donors hesitate due to missing proof of impact and tedious manual issuance of 80G tax exemption certificates.

05. AI Microservices Engine

AI Engine #1: Skill Vector Matcher

Automated pairing of volunteer capabilities with campaign operational demands.

Mathematical Match Formula

Calculates text overlap ratio between volunteer skills vector S_v and campaign requirement vector S_c, adjusted for location proximity boost.

Score = Min(99, Max(60, Base(65) + (OverlapRatio * 30) + (MatchCount * 5))) + 5% Location Proximity Boost (if Volunteer City == Campaign City)
  • ✓
    Match Score Breakdown: Gives exact skill alignment reasoning to volunteers before applying.

Sample FastAPI Execution

POST /ai/match-volunteer { "volunteer_skills": ["Teaching", "Web Development", "First Aid"], "volunteer_location": "New Delhi", "campaign_skills": ["Teaching", "Mentorship"], "campaign_location": "New Delhi" } // Response { "matchScore": 92, "matchLevel": "Excellent Match", "matchReason": "High skill alignment on: Teaching (Local proximity match)", "matchingSkills": ["teaching"], "locationProximity": true }
06. AI Microservices Engine

AI Engine #2: NLP Request Urgency Classifier

Scans beneficiary crisis descriptions to score request priority automatically.

Classification Rules & Weights

Utilizes keyword extraction & impact multipliers to compute urgency scores (25 to 99).

  • 🚨
    Emergency Keywords (+15 pts): critical, emergency, flood, disaster, starvation, icu, trauma
  • 🏥
    High-Priority Keywords (+10 pts): hospital, shelter, surgery, ration, treatment
  • 👥
    Population Multiplier: +20 pts for >20 affected people; +10 pts for >5 affected people.
